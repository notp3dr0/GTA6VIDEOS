## 2. Standard Daily Schedule (`America/Sao_Paulo` — BRT)

Minimum spacing rule on the same platform: **`>= 150 minutes`** (`2h30m`). Active window: **`08:30–22:30 BRT`**.

### YouTube Shorts (3 Standard / 4 Campaign Scaling Mode)
- **Standard Slots**: `11:30`, `16:30`, `20:30` BRT
- **Real Oficial 5-Day Scaling Slots**: `10:00`, `14:00`, `18:00`, `21:30` BRT
- **Mix**: 2–3 `real_oficial_campaign` acervo micro-cuts + 1 `spotlights` / `tips_tutorials` / `gta5_nostalgia`

### Instagram Reels (2 Daily)
- **Slots**: `12:00`, `19:30` BRT
- **Mix**: Slot 1 = `spotlights` or `tips_tutorials` (high save rate); Slot 2 = `gameplay_clips` or `gta5_nostalgia` (high share rate)

### TikTok (3 Daily)
- **Slots**: `11:45`, `17:00`, `21:00` BRT
- **Mix**: Fast-paced `official_news` detail, `gameplay_clips` physics moment, and `gta5_nostalgia` comparison

### X / Twitter (6 Daily — Zero Hashtags)
- `08:30` — `official_news` (morning verified briefing)
- `10:30` — `tips_tutorials` (actionable mechanic or setting tip)
- `13:00` — `gameplay_clips` (midday physics/detail clip)
- `15:30` — `gta5_nostalgia` (GTA V vs GTA VI comparison)
- `18:30` — `spotlights` (Leonida region or character spotlight)
- `21:30` — `countdown_recap` / `questions` (evening recap + community question)

## 3. Automatic Pre-Launch → Post-Launch Pivot (`2026-11-19`)
- **Before `2026-11-19` (Pre-Launch Mode)**:
  - Focus on Trailer 1 & Trailer 2 micro-details, official Rockstar regional spotlights, GTA V nostalgia, and pre-launch preparation tips.
  - Strictly block any leaked early copies or street-date breaks.
- **On & After `2026-11-19` (Post-Launch Leonida Mode)**:
  - `countdown_recap` automatically shifts to *"Dia X em Leonida"*.
  - Enable `spoiler_tag_required = true` (`⚠️ SPOILER`) on any narrative/ending clip while prioritizing spoiler-free open-world physics, easter eggs, and beginner tips for maximum safe reach.
