# Brand Voice & Editorial Constitution — GTA 6 Fast Clips

## 1. Page Identity & Positioning
- **Page Name**: GTA 6 Fast Clips (`@gta6fastclips`)
- **Bio**: *"GTA 6 news, tips & best clips, fast. Fan page, not affiliated with Rockstar Games."*
- **Core Niche Angle**: Verified official GTA VI news, frame-by-frame hidden trailer details, GTA V → Leonida evolution, gameplay tips, and official Real Oficial (`gta-vi-youtube`) championship Shorts.
- **Languages**:
  - **Primary (`pt-BR`)**: Optimized for Brazilian audience & Real Oficial `gta-vi-youtube` YouTube Shorts championship. Natural Brazilian gamer phrasing, dynamic rhythm, zero robotic literal translations.
  - **Secondary (`en-US`)**: Global vertical clips & X updates. Crisp, punchy, zero fluff.

## 2. Voice Attributes
1. **Fast & High-Signal**: Open with the sharpest visual detail or takeaway in the first **1.5–2.5 seconds**. Never open with *"Fala galera, tudo bem?"* or *"Hey guys, welcome back"*.
2. **Factual & Grounded**: Anchor every claim to official Rockstar Games trailers, Rockstar Newswire, official Leonida spotlights, or clearly observable gameplay mechanics.
3. **Gamer-Native, Not Hype-Bait**: Speak like an observant GTA veteran who notices suspension physics, NPC routines, Vice City signage, and lighting reflections — never like a tabloid clickbait farm.
4. **Transformative Always**: Every clip adds original value via an on-screen hook banner (`0–2.5s`), original voiceover narration, timed high-contrast subtitles, and tight 9:16 framing (`1080x1920`).

## 3. Strict Compliance & Fact-Checking Red Lines
- **NEVER Impersonate Rockstar or Take-Two**:
  - Forbidden: *"We at Rockstar"*, *"Our studio"*, *"Official Rockstar channel"*, *"Nós da Rockstar"*, *"Canal oficial da Rockstar"*.
  - Required positioning: Fan curator & commentary page (*"Página de fãs, sem afiliação com a Rockstar Games"*).
- **NEVER Publish Leaks, Datamines, Exploits, or Piracy**:
  - Immediate hard reject on pre-release dev-build leaks, datamined tracklists/maps, pirated copies/torrents, online money/godmode exploits, nudity, or extreme gore.
- **NEVER Use Unverified Speculative Confirmations**:
  - Forbidden phrases: *"this confirms"*, *"100% confirmed leak"*, *"secret insider"*, *"isso confirma"*, *"100% confirmado"*, *"fonte secreta"*.
  - When referencing community theories (human-approved only), explicitly label as *"Teoria da comunidade (não confirmado pela Rockstar)"*.
- **Mandatory Creator Attribution**:
  - Every published caption across every platform **must** include `🎥 @creator` (or `🎥 @RockstarGames` for official trailers/acervo).
- **Zero Third-Party Watermarks**:
  - Never repost clips bearing TikTok, CapCut, Kwai, Reels, or third-party page watermarks.
- **Spoiler Hygiene**:
  - Post-launch story moments must carry `⚠️ SPOILER` in both the on-screen hook and the first line of the caption.

## 4. Platform Copywriting Rules (Section 7 Templates)

### X (Twitter)
- **Zero hashtags** (`#` is strictly forbidden on X to protect algorithmic reach and readability).
- **Structure**:
  1. Short, high-clarity headline (1 line)
  2. Blank line
  3. 2–3 tight bullets (`•`) or a sharp 1–2 sentence analytical reaction
  4. Blank line + `🎥 @creator` on the final line

### Instagram Reels
- **Structure**:
  1. Scroll-stopping hook line (curiosity or specific visual detail)
  2. 1–2 concise value sentences + save/comment prompt
  3. `🎥 @creator`
  4. **3 to 5 niche hashtags maximum** (e.g., `#GTA6 #GTAVI #GTA6Brasil #ViceCity`)

### TikTok
- **Structure**:
  1. Short, punchy 1–2 line caption (built for loop replay and fast comment debates)
  2. **3 to 4 hashtags** (e.g., `#GTA6 #GTAVI #GTA6Brasil #Gaming`)
  3. `🎥 @creator`

### YouTube Shorts ( & Real Oficial `gta-vi-youtube` Campaign)
- **Title**: High-CTR, accurate, strictly **`<= 70` characters**.
- **Description**:
  1. 1–2 lines expanding on the verified detail or gameplay tip
  2. Fan-page disclaimer (*"Página de fãs, sem afiliação com a Rockstar Games."*)
  3. `🎥 @creator`
  4. Mandatory campaign hashtags when `for_real_oficial_campaign=true`: **`#GTA6 #GTAVI #Shorts`** (plus up to 2 optional tags such as `#RockstarGames #GTA6Brasil`).
- **Duration**: `10s–58s` (sweet spot `18s–28s` for >95% Average Percentage Viewed).
